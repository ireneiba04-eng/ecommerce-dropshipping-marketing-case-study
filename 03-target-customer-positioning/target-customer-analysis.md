# Target Customer & Positioning Analysis

## Product

Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream

Supplier platform: CJ Dropshipping

This analysis defines an initial target customer, identifies customer needs and pain points, and develops a preliminary positioning strategy for the selected product.

## Primary Target Customer

The initial target audience is adults approximately 25–45 years old who are interested in skincare and may experience dry or uncomfortable skin.

The audience includes both men and women. Skincare is not limited to one gender, and the initial strategy therefore avoids unnecessarily restricting the product to women.

The United States is the initial geographic market hypothesis, with other markets to be evaluated separately based on competition, demand, costs, and customer behavior.

## Customer Problem

Potential customers may experience:

- Dry skin

- Itchiness or discomfort associated with dryness

- Flakiness

- Tight or uncomfortable-feeling skin

- Concerns about maintaining hydrated and healthy-looking skin

Environmental conditions may also contribute to the problem. For example, people living in dry climates may have a greater need for regular moisturization.

## Desired Customer Outcome

Customers may be looking for:

- More hydrated-feeling skin

- Softer skin

- Reduced discomfort associated with dryness

- A simple everyday moisturizing solution

- An affordable skincare option

These outcomes are customer needs and hypotheses rather than validated product-performance claims.

## Purchase Drivers

Two primary purchase drivers were identified:

1. **Perceived effectiveness** — whether customers believe the product can address their need for everyday hydration and moisturization.

2. **Price/value** — whether the $24.99 selling price provides sufficient perceived value compared with competing products.

These factors require validation through competitor research, customer feedback, testing, and eventually conversion data.

## Positioning Hypothesis

Hoigi could be positioned as an affordable everyday moisturizer for consumers seeking hydration without paying a premium skincare price.

The positioning emphasizes:

- Everyday use

- Hydration and moisturization

- Affordability

- Value for money

- Broad appeal across genders

This is an initial positioning hypothesis and should be refined as customer and campaign data becomes available.

## Core Marketing Message

> **“Dry, itchy skin? Try Hoigi for everyday hydration at an affordable price.”**

The message follows a simple:

**Problem → Solution → Benefit → Value**

framework.

- **Problem:** Dry, itchy skin

- **Solution:** Hoigi

- **Benefit:** Everyday hydration

- **Value:** Affordable price

## Channel Hypothesis

Social platforms such as TikTok and Meta may be useful for product discovery and awareness.

Customers may discover the product through social content without immediately purchasing. Some customers may subsequently search for the product or brand through search engines before converting.

Therefore, the customer journey may involve multiple marketing touchpoints rather than a single channel.

Potential channels to evaluate include:

- TikTok

- Meta

- Google Search

- Retargeting

- Creator or influencer content

Attribution should be analyzed before assigning full conversion credit to any single channel.

## Validation Plan

The target customer and positioning should be validated through:

1. Competitor research

2. Customer and market research

3. Creative testing

4. Audience testing

5. Conversion-rate analysis

6. Customer feedback and reviews

7. CAC analysis

8. Attribution analysis

9. Incrementality testing where appropriate

## Current Status

The target customer and positioning have been developed as initial hypotheses.

No conclusion has been made that Hoigi will outperform competing products.

Further testing and evidence are required to determine whether the target audience, positioning, pricing, and acquisition strategy are commercially viable.
