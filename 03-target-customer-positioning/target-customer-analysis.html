03 · TARGET CUSTOMER & POSITIONING

Target Customer & Positioning Analysis

Defining the target customer, identifying customer needs and pain points, and developing a preliminary positioning strategy for sustainable e-commerce growth.

Product

Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream

Supplier platform: CJ Dropshipping

This analysis defines an initial target customer, identifies customer needs and pain points, and develops a preliminary positioning strategy for the selected product.

Primary Target Customer

The initial target audience is adults approximately 25–45 years old who are interested in skincare and may experience dry or uncomfortable skin.

The audience includes both men and women. Skincare is not limited to one gender, and the initial strategy therefore avoids unnecessarily restricting the product to women.

The United States is the initial geographic market hypothesis, with other markets to be evaluated separately based on competition, demand, costs, and customer behavior.

Customer Problem

Potential customers may experience:

  • Dry skin
  • Itchiness or discomfort associated with dryness
  • Flakiness
  • Tight or uncomfortable-feeling skin

These problems can create a need for a simple and convenient skincare solution that helps customers maintain comfortable, moisturized skin.

Customer Needs

  • Effective daily moisturization
  • Relief from the uncomfortable feeling associated with dry skin
  • A convenient skincare product that is easy to incorporate into a routine
  • A product that can appeal to a broad adult skincare audience
  • A clear value proposition relative to competing skincare products

Positioning Strategy

The product should initially be positioned as a practical moisturizing skincare solution for adults who want to address dryness and improve everyday skin comfort.

Core positioning idea:
Convenient everyday moisturization for adults seeking comfortable, hydrated-feeling skin.

Rather than positioning the product around an overly narrow demographic, the initial strategy focuses on the customer problem and desired outcome: comfortable, moisturized skin.

Targeting Rationale

The 25–45 age range provides a practical starting hypothesis because adults in this group may have established skincare routines while also experiencing lifestyle factors that can contribute to dry or uncomfortable skin.

Keeping the audience open to both men and women allows the initial marketing strategy to gather evidence before narrowing the audience based on actual performance data.

Audience assumptions should ultimately be validated through customer research, advertising data, conversion behavior, and retention performance.

Marketing Implications

  • Creative should emphasize the customer problem and desired outcome.
  • Ad messaging can focus on dryness, comfort, and daily moisturization.
  • Audience targeting should begin broad enough to collect meaningful data.
  • Performance data should be used to identify higher-value customer segments.
  • Landing-page messaging should remain consistent with the advertising promise.

Conclusion

The initial target customer is an adult skincare consumer, approximately 25–45 years old, who is interested in maintaining comfortable, moisturized skin.

The initial positioning focuses on solving a clear customer problem rather than relying solely on demographic targeting. Further testing should validate customer demand, messaging, audience segments, and conversion performance before making the strategy more restrictive.
