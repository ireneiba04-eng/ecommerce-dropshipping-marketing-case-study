Product Selection — Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream

Evaluating a potential e-commerce product through product-market considerations, supplier feasibility, customer demand, and fulfillment economics.

Product Overview

The selected product for this e-commerce case study is the Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream, identified through product research on CJ Dropshipping.

The product was selected as a candidate for further evaluation based on its positioning within the skincare and beauty category, potential customer demand, and the initial supplier and fulfillment information available through CJ Dropshipping.

Supplier Evaluation

CJ Dropshipping

CJ Dropshipping was selected as the supplier platform for this project after comparing available sourcing and fulfillment options.

The supplier evaluation focuses on whether the product can be sourced and fulfilled at a cost that supports sustainable customer acquisition and contribution margin.

Initial Product Information

Product Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream
Category Skincare / Beauty
Product Size 50 ml
Supplier Platform CJ Dropshipping

Research Approach

The product-selection stage is designed to determine whether the product is strong enough to justify deeper analysis before investing resources into acquisition and conversion optimization.

  • Evaluate the product category and market opportunity.
  • Assess supplier and fulfillment feasibility.
  • Consider potential customer demand.
  • Evaluate whether the economics can support paid acquisition.
  • Identify factors that should be investigated in later stages.

Decision

The Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream was selected as the working product for the case study and advanced to the next stage of analysis.

Product selection is treated as an initial hypothesis rather than proof of commercial viability. Further validation is required through unit economics, target-customer analysis, acquisition strategy, measurement, and conversion experimentation.
