# Paid Media Acquisition Strategy

## Product

Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream

## Objective

The objective of the initial paid-media test is to determine which acquisition channels can generate customers efficiently while preserving sustainable unit economics.

Because the initial advertising budget is limited, the strategy prioritizes controlled testing rather than aggressive scaling.

## Initial Budget

Total test budget: $500

The initial budget is divided between two paid-media channels:

- TikTok Ads: $300

- Meta Ads: $200

### Rationale

TikTok was selected because it can provide broad reach and is well suited to visual product discovery and short-form creative testing.

Meta was selected because it provides strong audience targeting and can support retargeting and conversion-focused campaigns.

Google Search, influencer marketing, and creator partnerships were not prioritized in the initial test because the available budget is limited. These channels can be evaluated later if the initial economics support expansion.

## Initial Hypothesis

The initial hypothesis is that TikTok can efficiently generate awareness and new customer demand, while Meta may provide stronger conversion efficiency and retargeting opportunities.

However, channel performance should be evaluated using actual conversion and profitability data rather than assumptions about which platform is inherently better.

## Hypothetical Test Results

### TikTok

Advertising spend: $300

Customers acquired: 15

CAC:

$300 / 15 = $20 per customer

### Meta

Advertising spend: $200

Customers acquired: 20

CAC:

$200 / 20 = $10 per customer

## Initial Interpretation

Based on the hypothetical results, Meta generated customers at a lower reported CAC than TikTok.

- TikTok CAC: $20

- Meta CAC: $10

Meta therefore appears more efficient on a simple customer-acquisition basis.

However, this result alone is not sufficient to conclude that Meta should receive the majority of the future budget.

## Attribution Analysis

Before reallocating the budget, the customer journey should be investigated.

A customer may interact with multiple marketing touchpoints before purchasing.

For example:

TikTok impression → TikTok engagement → website visit → later Meta interaction → purchase

In this situation, assigning the entire conversion to Meta could overstate Meta's contribution while understating TikTok's contribution.

The analysis should therefore examine:

- First-touch attribution

- Last-touch attribution

- Multi-touch attribution where appropriate

- Cross-channel customer journeys

- Assisted conversions

- Retargeting interactions

- Conversion paths and time between touchpoints

Attribution should be analyzed before assigning full conversion credit to a single channel.

## Incrementality

Attribution alone does not determine whether advertising caused an incremental conversion.

Incrementality should be evaluated where practical to determine whether customers would have purchased without the additional advertising exposure.

For example, a retargeting campaign may receive conversion credit from customers who were already highly likely to purchase.

Therefore, strong reported CAC does not automatically mean that the channel generated the same amount of incremental demand.

## Budget Decision Framework

The initial results suggest that Meta deserves further investigation because its reported CAC is lower.

However, I would not immediately move the entire budget from TikTok to Meta.

Before reallocating significantly, I would investigate:

1. Conversion paths and attribution.

2. Audience differences between the two platforms.

3. Creative performance.

4. Landing-page behavior.

5. Conversion rate by channel.

6. Incremental conversions.

7. Customer quality and repeat-purchase behavior.

8. Contribution margin after acquisition costs.

If Meta continues to demonstrate stronger incremental performance and sustainable economics, the budget can gradually shift toward Meta.

TikTok would not necessarily be eliminated because it may contribute valuable awareness and assist conversions that are later attributed to another channel.

## Marginal Budgeting

Budget increases should be made incrementally rather than all at once.

For example, if a channel performs efficiently at a $200 budget, the next step could be a controlled increase rather than immediately doubling or tripling spend.

After each increase, performance should be evaluated using:

- Marginal CAC

- Conversion rate

- Contribution margin

- Incremental conversions

- Marginal ROAS

- Customer quality

A channel that performs well at a small budget may experience declining efficiency as spend increases.

Therefore, scalability must be demonstrated rather than assumed.

## Initial Recommendation

The initial recommendation is to continue testing both TikTok and Meta while collecting additional data.

Meta currently shows the lower reported CAC:

$10 vs. $20

However, the appropriate decision is not simply to move all spending to Meta.

The next decision should be based on attribution, incrementality, marginal CAC, contribution margin, and the sustainability of performance as the budget increases.

The preferred approach is controlled experimentation followed by incremental budget reallocation based on evidence.

## Current Status

The acquisition strategy remains in the testing stage.

The initial results suggest that Meta may be more efficient for direct customer acquisition, while TikTok may contribute to awareness and assisted conversions.

No final channel winner has been declared because additional evidence is required to determine true incremental performance and scalability.
