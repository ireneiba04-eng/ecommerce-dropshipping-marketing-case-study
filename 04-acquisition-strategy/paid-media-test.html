Paid Media Acquisition Strategy

Designing a controlled paid-media test to identify efficient acquisition channels while protecting sustainable unit economics.

Product

Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream

The paid-media strategy is designed around the selected skincare product and the target customer identified in the previous analysis.

Objective

The objective of the initial paid-media test is to determine which acquisition channels can generate customers efficiently while preserving sustainable unit economics.

Because the initial advertising budget is limited, the strategy prioritizes controlled testing rather than aggressive scaling.

Strategic principle: Validate channel performance and conversion economics before increasing spend.

Initial Budget

The total initial test budget is $500. The budget is divided between two paid-media channels:

Channel Rationale

TikTok Ads

TikTok was selected because it can provide broad reach and is well suited to visual product discovery and short-form creative testing.

Meta Ads

Meta was selected because it provides strong audience targeting and can support retargeting and conversion-focused campaigns.

Channels Not Prioritized Initially

Google Search, influencer marketing, and creator partnerships were not prioritized in the initial test because the available budget is limited. These channels can be evaluated in later testing if the initial results justify expansion.

Testing Approach

Phase 1 — Controlled Test

Launch both channels with clearly defined budgets and consistent conversion tracking. The purpose of this phase is to establish baseline performance rather than maximize short-term volume.

Primary Metrics

  • Click-through rate (CTR)
  • Cost per click (CPC)
  • Conversion rate
  • Cost per acquisition (CPA)
  • Return on ad spend (ROAS)
  • Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

Decision Framework

Budget should be shifted toward the channel that demonstrates stronger conversion efficiency and healthier unit economics, rather than simply the channel generating the most clicks or impressions.

A channel should not be scaled solely because it produces inexpensive traffic. Conversion quality, CPA, and downstream profitability must be considered before increasing spend.

Optimization Strategy

  • Test multiple creative concepts before scaling spend.
  • Compare audience segments based on conversion performance.
  • Monitor landing-page performance alongside ad performance.
  • Pause inefficient combinations rather than allowing them to consume budget.
  • Increase spend gradually when performance remains stable.
Scaling rule: Increase budget only after the campaign demonstrates repeatable conversion performance and acceptable acquisition economics.

Key Risks & Controls

  • Limited budget: Use controlled experiments and avoid premature scaling.
  • Creative fatigue: Prepare multiple creative variations and monitor engagement trends.
  • Low-quality traffic: Evaluate conversion behavior rather than relying only on CTR.
  • Unprofitable acquisition: Compare CAC and CPA against product-level economics before increasing spend.

Expected Outcome

The initial test is designed to identify which paid-media channel provides the strongest combination of reach, conversion efficiency, and sustainable acquisition economics.

The results will inform future budget allocation, creative testing, audience refinement, retargeting strategy, and eventual campaign scaling.

Conclusion

The acquisition strategy uses a controlled $500 test across TikTok and Meta rather than committing heavily to a single channel before sufficient performance data is available.

The strategy emphasizes measurement, experimentation, and unit economics so that future spending decisions can be based on evidence rather than assumptions.
