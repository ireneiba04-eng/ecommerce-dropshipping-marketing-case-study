# E-Commerce & Digital Marketing Case Study

## Overview

A hands-on e-commerce project designed to apply digital marketing concepts in a real-world business environment.

This project documents the process of researching, evaluating, launching, measuring, and optimizing an e-commerce business using data-driven marketing principles.

## Business Objective

Build and evaluate a low-cost e-commerce business while developing practical skills in:

- Product research

- Competitor analysis

- Supplier evaluation

- Unit economics

- Customer acquisition

- Paid media

- Conversion rate optimization

- Marketing analytics

- Attribution

- Incrementality

- Experimentation

- Performance measurement

- Budget allocation

- Strategic decision-making

## Product Research

The project began with product and supplier research, including evaluation of:

- Product demand

- Competitive pricing

- Supplier pricing

- Shipping and fulfillment

- Processing and delivery times

- Product positioning

- Customer value

- Unit economics

## Supplier Evaluation

Suppliers are evaluated based on:

- Product cost

- Shipping cost

- Fulfillment

- Processing time

- Delivery time

- Product specifications

- Taxes and fees

- Competitive pricing

- Overall profitability potential

## Marketing Analysis

The project will evaluate:

- CTR — Click-Through Rate

- CPC — Cost Per Click

- CPM — Cost Per Mille

- CVR — Conversion Rate

- CPA — Cost Per Acquisition/Action

- CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost

- ROAS — Return on Ad Spend

- AOV — Average Order Value

- Contribution Margin

- LTV — Lifetime Value

- Marginal CAC

- Marginal ROAS

## Customer Acquisition

Marketing channels and acquisition strategies will be evaluated based on:

- Acquisition efficiency

- Customer quality

- Conversion performance

- CAC

- Contribution after CAC

- Scalability

- Incrementality

- Attribution

## Optimization Process

The project follows a data-driven optimization framework:

1. Research

2. Form a hypothesis

3. Test

4. Measure

5. Analyze

6. Optimize

7. Re-measure

8. Make a business recommendation

## Key Learning Objectives

The goal of this project is not simply to generate sales.

It is to develop practical experience in using data to make marketing and business decisions.

Key questions include:

- Can the selected product compete with existing alternatives?

- Can the product be sourced and fulfilled profitably?

- Who is the target customer?

- What is the appropriate positioning?

- Which acquisition channels are most effective?

- What is the CAC?

- What is the CPA?

- Is the contribution margin sufficient?

- Is customer acquisition sustainable?

- Can the business scale efficiently?
