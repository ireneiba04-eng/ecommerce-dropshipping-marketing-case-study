Measurement & Analytics Framework

Designing a measurement system that connects paid-media activity to customer acquisition efficiency, profitability, and scalable growth.

Planning stage · No campaign data yet

Business Context

This measurement framework was designed for the Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream ecommerce case study. The goal is to establish how marketing performance will be measured before campaign data is available.

Product Hoigi Moisturizing Skin Cream
Primary conversion Completed purchase
Core objective Evaluate acquisition efficiency and profitability
Decision principle Connect marketing metrics to business outcomes

Measurement Objective

The objective is to create a consistent framework for evaluating marketing performance, customer acquisition efficiency, profitability, and scalability.

Individual metrics should not be evaluated in isolation. A strong measurement system should show how traffic and advertising activity ultimately influence purchases, acquisition cost, revenue, and contribution margin.

Analytical principle: The purpose of measurement is not simply to report numbers. It is to turn performance data into better marketing and budget decisions.

Key Performance Indicators

Impressions Measures ad exposure and reach. Useful for understanding delivery, but does not indicate engagement or purchase intent.
Click-Through Rate (CTR) Measures the percentage of impressions that result in clicks and helps evaluate creative and audience relevance.
Cost per Click (CPC) Measures the average cost of generating a click and helps assess traffic acquisition efficiency.
Conversion Rate Measures the percentage of relevant visits that result in the primary conversion: a completed purchase.
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Measures the acquisition cost required to generate a customer and connects media spend to customer economics.
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) Measures attributed revenue relative to advertising spend. Useful for comparing campaign efficiency, but should not be treated as profit.
Contribution Margin Evaluates the economics remaining after relevant variable costs and acquisition costs.
Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Provides a longer-term view of customer value as sufficient repeat-purchase data becomes available.

Core Calculations

Click-Through Rate

CTR = Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100

Conversion Rate

Conversion Rate = Purchases ÷ Relevant Sessions × 100

Customer Acquisition Cost

CAC = Acquisition Spend ÷ New Customers

Return on Ad Spend

ROAS = Attributed Revenue ÷ Advertising Spend

These calculations provide consistent definitions for reporting and make channel-level comparisons more reliable.

Measurement Architecture

The measurement system should connect advertising platforms, website behavior, ecommerce conversions, and reporting into one analytical workflow.

Layer Purpose Examples
Ad platforms Measure media delivery and spend. Impressions, clicks, CPC, spend
Website analytics Measure user behavior after the click. Sessions, engagement, landing-page behavior
Ecommerce Measure business conversions. Purchases, revenue, customer data
Reporting Combine performance data for decision making. Channel performance, CAC, ROAS, margin

Campaign & UTM Tracking

Campaign links should use a consistent naming convention so traffic can be grouped and analyzed by source, medium, campaign, and creative.

utm_source = platform
utm_medium = paid_social / paid_search
utm_campaign = campaign_name
utm_content = creative_or_ad_variant

Consistent tracking reduces ambiguity when comparing acquisition channels and helps connect campaign traffic to downstream website and purchase behavior.

Attribution Approach

Attribution should be treated as a measurement model rather than a perfect representation of causality. Different platforms may report conversions differently because they use different attribution windows and rules.

Initial approach

  • Track the purchase event consistently.
  • Use campaign and UTM data to identify acquisition sources.
  • Compare platform-reported conversions with analytics and ecommerce data.
  • Investigate differences instead of assuming every reported conversion is incremental.

Incrementality

Where practical, incremental testing should be used to determine whether additional advertising spend actually creates additional demand rather than simply receiving credit for conversions that may have happened anyway.

Decision Framework

Scale If acquisition efficiency remains healthy and contribution margin supports additional spend, consider increasing budget gradually.
Optimize If traffic is strong but conversion efficiency is weak, investigate targeting, creative, landing-page experience, or offer alignment.
Restrict If marginal CAC rises materially as spend increases, the channel may be approaching saturation.
Investigate If platform reporting and business outcomes disagree, investigate tracking, attribution, and incrementality before reallocating budget.

Measurement Priorities

  1. Track purchases accurately.
  2. Measure CTR and CPC by channel.
  3. Measure website conversion rate.
  4. Calculate CAC by channel.
  5. Calculate attributed ROAS.
  6. Evaluate contribution margin after acquisition costs.
  7. Analyze customer journeys and attribution.
  8. Evaluate incrementality where practical.
  9. Monitor marginal CAC and marginal ROAS during budget increases.
  10. Evaluate LTV as sufficient customer data becomes available.

Current Status & Next Steps

The measurement framework has been defined as an initial analytical plan. Actual campaign data is not yet available, so this case study intentionally does not present fabricated performance results.

Next analytical phase: Once campaigns generate sufficient data, the framework can be used to compare channels, diagnose funnel performance, evaluate acquisition economics, and guide budget allocation.

What I would analyze after launch

  • Channel-level CAC and ROAS
  • Creative and audience performance
  • Landing-page and website conversion rate
  • Marginal CAC as budgets increase
  • Contribution margin after acquisition costs
  • Attribution differences across platforms and analytics
  • Early signals of repeat purchase and LTV
